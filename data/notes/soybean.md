# Soybean (सोयाबीन) — kharif notes for Maharashtra

Teaching data. Typical ranges from public extension advice; confirm with your local Krishi Vigyan Kendra before acting.

## Sowing window
- Sow after the monsoon has delivered 75–100 mm of rain and the soil is moist to 15 cm. In most of Vidarbha and Marathwada this falls between 15 June and 7 July.
- Sowing after 15 July cuts yield sharply because the crop flowers in a shortening day.
- Do not sow into dry soil hoping for rain; germination fails if the top layer dries.

## Seed and seed rate
- Seed rate: 65–75 kg per hectare (about 26–30 kg per acre) for medium-seeded varieties such as JS-335 and JS 93-05. Use the higher end for bold-seeded varieties like MAUS-71.
- Germination test before sowing: at least 70 of 100 seeds should sprout in a wet cloth within 5 days. Raise the seed rate in proportion if germination is 60–70 percent; do not use seed below 60 percent.
- Seed treatment order: fungicide first (Thiram plus Carbendazim, 3 g per kg of seed), then Rhizobium and PSB cultures (250 g each per 10 kg of seed). Sow within 6 hours of treating with cultures.

## Spacing and depth
- Rows 45 cm apart, plants 5 cm apart within the row. Depth 3–4 cm. Deeper than 5 cm delays emergence.
- On heavy black soil, sow on broad bed and furrow (BBF) so excess water drains; waterlogging for two days at flowering can cost a third of the yield.

## Nutrients
- Basal dose per hectare: 30 kg N, 60 kg P2O5, 30 kg K2O and 20 kg sulphur. Sulphur through gypsum or single super phosphate improves oil content.
- Apply all fertiliser at sowing. Top dressing nitrogen is not recommended for soybean.

## Weeds
- The first 30–45 days are critical. One hand weeding at 20 days and a second at 40 days, or a pre-emergence herbicide within 2 days of sowing followed by one weeding.

## Pests and diseases
- Stem fly and girdle beetle: watch from 15 days after emergence; remove and destroy wilted plants.
- Spodoptera (tobacco caterpillar) and semilooper: scout in the evening; treat when 3–4 larvae per metre of row are found.
- Yellow mosaic virus is spread by whitefly. Uproot infected plants early and use resistant varieties in affected areas.

## Harvest
- Ready when 90–95 percent of pods have turned brown and most leaves have dropped, usually 95–105 days after sowing. Harvest at 15–17 percent seed moisture and dry to 10–12 percent for storage.
- Expected yield in a good year: 18–25 quintals per hectare.
