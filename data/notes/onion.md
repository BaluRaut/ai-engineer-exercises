# Onion (कांदा) — kharif notes for Maharashtra

Teaching data. Typical ranges from public extension advice; confirm with your local Krishi Vigyan Kendra before acting.

## Seasons
- Kharif onion: nursery sown mid May to mid June, transplanted July to early August, harvested October to November. Prices are usually highest in this window because supply is short.
- Late kharif (rangda): nursery August to September, transplanted September to October, harvested January to February.
- Rabi: nursery October to November, transplanted December to January, harvested April to May; this is the crop that stores best.

## Varieties
- Kharif: Bhima Super, Bhima Red, Baswant 780, Phule Samarth. Dark red, poor storage, so plan to sell within a month of harvest.
- Rabi for storage: Bhima Kiran, Bhima Shakti, N-2-4-1.

## Seed and nursery
- Seed rate: 8–10 kg per hectare of transplanted field. Raise the nursery on raised beds 1 m wide and 15 cm high, about 0.05 hectare of nursery for every hectare to be transplanted.
- Treat seed with Thiram (2 g per kg). Cover beds with paddy straw until emergence, then remove.
- Seedlings are ready for transplanting at 6–7 weeks, when they are pencil thick and 15 cm tall.

## Transplanting
- Spacing 15 cm between rows and 10 cm between plants on flat beds or broad beds. Transplant in the evening and irrigate at once.
- In kharif, broad beds with furrows every 1.2 m are essential; onion cannot stand waterlogging even for a day.

## Nutrients
- Per hectare: 100 kg N, 50 kg P2O5, 50 kg K2O and 30 kg sulphur. Half the N at transplanting, a quarter at 30 days, a quarter at 45 days. No nitrogen after 60 days, or bulbs will not cure well.

## Pests and diseases
- Thrips: silvery streaks on leaves; most damaging in dry spells. Spray at threshold, alternate chemicals.
- Purple blotch and anthracnose (twister) in the rains: start protective sprays at the first sign, remove infected leaves.
- Stemphylium blight in late kharif; avoid overhead sprinkler irrigation.

## Harvest and curing
- Kharif bulbs are ready when 50 percent of tops fall over, about 100–110 days after transplanting. Cure in the shade for 3–5 days with tops on, then cut tops leaving 2–3 cm of neck.
- Expected kharif yield: 150–200 quintals per hectare.
