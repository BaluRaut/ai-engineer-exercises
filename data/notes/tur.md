# Tur / pigeonpea (तूर) — kharif notes for Maharashtra

Teaching data. Typical ranges from public extension advice; confirm with your local Krishi Vigyan Kendra before acting.

## Sowing window
- Sow with the first good monsoon rain, between 15 June and 15 July. Every week of delay after early July reduces yield because the plant has fewer growing days before it flowers.
- Sole crop or intercrop: the usual Marathwada pattern is soybean and tur in a 4:2 or 6:3 row arrangement, so the tur keeps growing after soybean is harvested.

## Seed and seed rate
- Sole crop: 12–15 kg per hectare for medium-duration varieties such as BDN-711, BSMR-736 and Godavari; 18–20 kg for short-duration ones.
- As an intercrop with soybean, tur seed drops to 4–5 kg per hectare.
- Treat seed with Trichoderma (4 g per kg) against wilt, then Rhizobium and PSB cultures.

## Spacing
- Medium duration: 90 cm between rows, 20 cm between plants. On deep black soil use 120 cm by 30 cm. Short duration: 60 cm by 20 cm.
- Depth 3–5 cm. Thin to one plant per hill at 15 days.

## Nutrients
- Per hectare at sowing: 25 kg N, 50 kg P2O5. Tur fixes its own nitrogen after the first month, so no top dressing.
- Two sprays of 2 percent urea or DAP at flowering and pod setting help under dry conditions.

## Water
- Mostly rain-fed. Where possible, one protective irrigation at flowering and one at pod filling can double the yield in a drought year.

## Pests and diseases
- Pod borer (Helicoverpa) is the main pest: shake plants over a cloth to count larvae, install pheromone traps, and spray at flowering if 1–2 larvae per plant are found. Include one neem-based spray in the sequence.
- Pod fly: damages later pods; treat at pod set.
- Wilt and sterility mosaic: use resistant varieties (BDN-711, BSMR-736), do not sow in the same field for three years.

## Harvest
- Ready when 75–80 percent of pods have dried, usually 160–180 days for medium-duration types. Cut plants, dry, thresh, and dry seed to 10 percent moisture.
- Expected yield: 12–18 quintals per hectare sole crop, 5–7 quintals as an intercrop.
