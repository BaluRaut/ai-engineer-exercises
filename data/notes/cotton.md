# Cotton (कापूस) — kharif notes for Maharashtra

Teaching data. Typical ranges from public extension advice; confirm with your local Krishi Vigyan Kendra before acting.

## Sowing window
- Rain-fed cotton: sow after 75–100 mm of monsoon rain, normally in the last week of June. Sowing in dry soil in May ("dry sowing") is common in some districts but risks poor stand if rain is late.
- Irrigated cotton: can be sown in the first fortnight of June with one pre-sowing irrigation.
- Late sowing after 15 July invites pink bollworm and lowers yield.

## Seed and seed rate
- Bt hybrids: one packet of 450 g per acre (about 2.5 packets per hectare). Plant one seed per hill; keep 10 percent extra seed for gap filling within 10 days.
- Non-Bt refuge: sow the refuge seed supplied with the packet around the field border, or use the refuge-in-bag mix as sold.
- Treat seed with Imidacloprid (5 g per kg) if not pre-treated, to protect against early sucking pests.

## Spacing
- Medium soil, rain-fed: 90 cm between rows and 60 cm between plants (about 18,500 plants per hectare).
- Deep black soil, irrigated: 120 cm by 60 cm to allow the plant to spread.
- High density planting (60 cm by 15 cm) with compact varieties is being tried on shallow soils; it needs strict growth regulation.

## Nutrients
- Rain-fed Bt cotton per hectare: 60 kg N, 30 kg P2O5, 30 kg K2O. Give one third of N at sowing, one third at 30 days, one third at 60 days.
- Magnesium sulphate 25 kg per hectare corrects the leaf reddening seen on many Vidarbha soils.
- Foliar spray of 2 percent urea or DAP at square and boll stage helps under moisture stress.

## Pests
- Sucking pests (jassid, aphid, thrips, whitefly) in the first 60 days: use yellow sticky traps and spray only when economic threshold is crossed (for jassid, 2–3 per leaf on the top three leaves).
- Pink bollworm from flowering onward: set 5 pheromone traps per hectare from 45 days, remove rosette flowers, and never keep the crop past 150 days. Destroy stalks after harvest; do not store them near fields.
- Mealybug: spot spray infested plants first, do not blanket spray.

## Harvest
- First picking around 120–130 days when 40 percent of bolls have opened. Pick in the morning after dew has dried; keep trash and immature bolls separate.
- Expected rain-fed yield: 8–12 quintals of seed cotton per hectare; irrigated can reach 20 or more.
